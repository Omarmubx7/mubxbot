# PURPOSE

Description: MUBXBot is a free web assistant for students of Al Hussein Technical University (HTU). It answers plain-language questions about instructor emails, office hours, office locations and departments using public directory data, with typo-tolerant fuzzy search.

Capabilities:
- Instructor email and contact lookup ("what" + instructor name)
- Office hours lookup ("when" + instructor name)
- Office location lookup ("where" + instructor name)
- Department search (list instructors by department)
- Typo-tolerant fuzzy search (Fuse.js)

Data Sources:
- Public instructor directory (doctors.json)
- Public office hours dataset (office_hours.json)
